FICTIONAL EXAMPLE — Full Stack Software Developer, Workflow Products

A small B2B software team is hiring a developer to build workflow and AI-assisted
features for web applications. The role is remote-first and based in Morocco.

Responsibilities:
- Build accessible React or Next.js interfaces and Python or FastAPI services.
- Design and integrate APIs backed by PostgreSQL.
- Turn product requirements into prototypes, then ship tested features.
- Maintain Docker-based development and CI/CD delivery workflows.
- Collaborate with product and design colleagues on customer-facing SaaS tools.

Requirements:
- At least three years of professional software development experience.
- Experience with React, Python, SQL, API integration, and automated testing.
- A computer science degree or equivalent practical experience.
- Clear written communication in English.

Nice to have:
- Experience with AWS, Kubernetes, TypeScript, and creator-marketing products.
